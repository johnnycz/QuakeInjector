Material Symbols
----------------

These icons are from Material Symbols:

  src/main/resources/delete_icon.svg     the "delete" icon (Uninstall button)
  src/main/resources/download_icon.svg   the "download" icon (Install button)
  src/main/resources/play_icon.svg       the "play_arrow" icon (Play button)

  Copyright Google LLC
  Source:  https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
  License: Apache License, Version 2.0 (see LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt,
           or https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)

The files are unchanged. The program draws them in the current theme's text color.
